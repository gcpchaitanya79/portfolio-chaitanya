About

I started as a Java developer on payment and billing systems for Citi Bank and AT&T. Over time I moved into data engineering, and I now build end-to-end pipelines on Google Cloud: moving on-premise databases into BigQuery, streaming events through Pub/Sub and Dataflow, and running large PySpark jobs on Dataproc and Databricks.

Because of that Java background, I can also build the services around the data: Spring Boot microservices, Kafka-based transaction tracking, and moving monolithic applications to microservices using the SAGA and Strangler patterns.

I have worked in banking, payments, telecom, retail analytics, supply chain and trade finance. I follow Agile/Scrum with TDD/BDD, set Spark development standards for my teams, and mentor junior engineers.
